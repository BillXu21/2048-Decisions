# MLP behavioral cloning and PPO

## Scratch PPO and hard-label imitation

The first GPU-resident PPO used a 16→128→128 shared ReLU MLP with masked four-action logits and a pre-action value head (19,333 total parameters). From scratch it completed 52,428,800 transitions / 200 updates, achieving 4,429.7 mean versus 1,078.3 random on 1,024 held-out games, with 3.7% reaching 1024 and none 2048. `ppo_gpu/runs/main/checkpoint_00200.pkl` was selected on separate validation seeds. 8.20M transitions/s was a simulator benchmark; 1.61M/s excluded compile/host overhead and the main-run wall rate was 215,608/s. None is a rate for full-D4 CNN PPO.

`bc_imitation` rebuilt hard action labels from 224 complete search games (200 at 10 ms/depth 3, 24 at 100 ms/depth 6; 320,667 transitions). The episode split was 200 training / 24 validation (282,460 / 38,207 rows). The 128×128 BC actor scored 3,501.8 greedy and a separately TTA-selected actor scored 3,738.1 on 1,024 held-out games; the latter's paired deficit vs PPO was −691.6 [−896.7, −487.2]. This motivated label/distribution hypotheses, not PPO fine-tuning of that old actor.

## Candidate-afterstate scoring, soft targets and scale

On a controlled data/optimizer/seed comparison, board-action MLP, CNN and shared candidate-afterstate scorer scored about 3,463, 7,641 and 13,548, respectively, on the ablation's held-out games (see `REPORT_ABLATION.md` for modes). The afterstate scorer used 16 exponent features plus log merge reward and empty fraction, a 256×256 MLP, and one scalar per candidate. This scalar was a **policy logit**, not a calibrated TD value. Mask illegal slides, transform candidate/action correspondences correctly, then average scores, not chosen moves. Hard search labels have tie-break and deadline noise (88.1% nominally repeated action agreement on 20k states). Soft targets use within-state softmax of legal search action values at T=32; do not treat those values as absolute returns. A bounded DAgger round gave no resolved gain versus reference. Soft afterstate BC reached 17,102.2 TTA on 1,024 held-out games; an independent historical repeat on those seeds gave 16,394.5 TTA but matched greedy 13,581.1, motivating one-process comparisons.

`overnight_afterstate` held the 61,042-state stratified student-visited pool fixed while varying weak/strong search supervision and 256×256/512×512 capacity. Base rows stayed hard-labelled. Adam 1e−3, clip 1, batch 4096, 80-epoch budget, sampled D4 augmentation; development gameplay (128-game screen, 512-game re-rank) preceded 1,024 fresh paired games. The capacity main effect was +2,142.9 [1,618.5, 2,662.9]; stronger supervision −26.7 [−560.8, +503.3]; one bounded self-generated round +24.3 [−723.0, +782.9]. An initial pool collector admitted finished-game zero rows and was replaced after audit. Retained arm A: weak targets, 512×512 / 272,898 params, epoch 80, 18,410.0 TTA and 15,225.6 single on this packet's final set. This is the MLP later fine-tuned, not the first board-action BC actor.

## Afterstate MLP PPO: 25M and continuation

Actor initialized from arm A; separate pre-action CNN critic with detached actor-score features. Empirical full-return warm targets were less suited to the short GAE horizon than truncated-50 returns. Full eight-way D4 policy in rollout/update; 512 environments × 128 steps for main runs, 3 epochs, clip 0.15, KL stop 0.02, γ=1, λ=.98, merge reward /128, value in score/12,800 units. Four 25M arms share one seed. At 25M the two reference-KL coefficient-1 arms scored 20.3–20.7k TTA on 256 development games versus 40,302.2 (R1 T=.5, no reference KL) and 38,426.6 (R3 T=1, no reference KL). R3 won the fresh selection comparison: 39,318.6 TTA, 15,988.4 single, +20,660.2 paired vs frozen BC [19,340.4, 21,916.8], 82.32%/30.66% reach at 2048/4096 on 1,024 fresh games.

Continuation started at 25,034,752, stopped at 50,003,968, selected **45,023,232**. Development TTA at 30M/35M/40M/45M/50M was 42,628.6 / 45,502.2 / 43,456.1 / 47,465.5 / 45,215.1 as aligned in the corrected milestone table. Corrected single-orientation candidate series was 11,036.8 / 8,156.2 / 7,883.4 / 7,699.7 / 6,271.6. The fresh 1,024-game confirmation used the 45M checkpoint: 46,405.8 TTA vs 39,669.9 for 25M, +6,735.9 [4,916.1, 8,604.6]; single 7,484.5 vs 15,988.4, −8,503.8 [−9,197.2, −7,835.3]. The 45M reach rates were 86.43% at 2048 and 45.12% at 4096. The 50M endpoint was never confirmed on those seeds. Early prose misattributed the result and suggested monotonic improvement/100M; the later audit corrected it. [V1/V2 controls](ntuple_v1_v2.md) show a small, protocol-sensitive continuation effect, not a known asymptote. No 100M run occurred.
