# Search baseline and early learned critics

The original snake-search policy used iterative deepening expectiminimax: MAX chose a legal slide, MIN chose the worst empty cell, and chance enumerated tile 2/4 with probabilities 0.9/0.1. Alpha–beta was confined to the MAX/MIN logic. The leaf heuristic evaluated snake orderings across rotations and reflections. A deadline returned the last completed depth or a legal one-ply fallback. This adversarial **planning** assumption is distinct from actual gameplay and data collection, which sample a uniform empty cell. The academic submission and evaluator materials are not part of this repository.

The teacher collected complete trajectories with state, deterministic post-slide/pre-spawn afterstate, action and merge reward. A pre-action score-to-go includes the current reward; an afterstate label excludes it and is zero when no future move is possible. Split by episode, not by individual board, to avoid within-game leakage. For action comparisons, add each candidate's immediate reward to its estimated future value. Unchosen moves have no observed counterfactual return label.

## Learned search leaves: promising development, tie in confirmation

The score-to-go MLP replaced the leaf evaluator without removing search. Regression alone ranked moves poorly (reported one-ply teacher agreement about 35–41%, versus about 73% for the heuristic); adding pairwise ranking lifted that diagnostic to roughly 57–73%. The primary ranked afterstate critic had a better 10-game development result by the best-five-max-tile metric (2,867.2 versus 2,457.6 for the 140 ms baseline), but the 10 fresh equal-100-ms/two-worker games tied at 2,457.6 on that metric. Mean score favored snake: 31,306 versus 29,594. The baseline was retained. The early neural work also reports an offline TD(lambda) ablation; this is not the later online TD program.

The JAX rank-loss port initially omitted move reward from the ranking comparison. Fixing this improved ranking but did not make the 128×128 leaf competitive within the same 100 ms CPU budget: 25,619.6 mean versus 29,593.6 for the earlier critic and 31,305.6 for snake. Wider search critics were not pursued at that budget; inference cost reduced reached depth. Later work instead learned a search-free neural move scorer. The later 35 ms snake vs MLP PPO comparison stopped after 8 of 16 games; its report's summary and displayed rows do not fully agree. No confirmed head-to-head win is asserted.
